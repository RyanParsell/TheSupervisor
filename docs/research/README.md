Material in this folder is research that will be used to help craft future plans.

## Naming

| | Research |
|---|---|
| Document | `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` |

Research is not a plan and is never implemented directly. A research document graduates by being
cited in a plan's grounding — or by being superseded and left in place as a record of what was
considered.

**Living docs** are the one exception to the date-prefix rule: a document that is appended to over
time (an ideas backlog, an ongoing investigation) may be named `<slug>.md` and carries a
`**Status:** Living doc` line under its title, with the date it was started.
